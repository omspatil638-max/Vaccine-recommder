GOAL
----
Turn the dashboard-first prototype into a guided vaccination decision and discovery
platform: login -> intake wizard -> review -> deterministic assessment -> explained
recommendations -> vaccine details -> providers/availability/cost -> action ->
after-care -> reminders/history -> home (overview only).

KEY DECISIONS
-------------
- 37 requested phases were compressed into 12; the user asked for no confirmations and no git.
- Jurisdictions: United States (CDC adult schedule) and India (MoHFW UIP; only Td in
  pregnancy, because the national programme has no routine schedule for other adults).
  Other countries get an honest "no guideline" result. Countries are never mixed.
- Adults only (18+ can sign up; the US schedule covers 19+). Children are out of scope.
- Medical decisions come from a deterministic, data-driven rule engine. No LLM is used
  anywhere (the optional "AI explanation" layer was deliberately left out: nothing
  about it would be verifiable offline, and the rule-based explanations already cover
  "Why am I seeing this?").
- Unknown is preserved everywhere: yes / no / not sure; "not sure" never becomes "no";
  unknown vaccination history -> "More information needed", never "never vaccinated".
- Precedence (safest first): up to date > contraindication > professional review >
  more information needed > recommended / may be appropriate.
- Vaccine ingredient allergies: only components named by the source (yeast for
  hepatitis B) produce a contraindication. A severe allergy to other possible vaccine
  ingredients (egg, gelatin, neomycin, polysorbate, latex) sends the vaccine for
  professional review instead of guessing.
- Provider/availability/price data is DEMO data, labelled everywhere. No fake booking.
- Free, local stack: FastAPI + SQLite (SQLModel), React 19 + Vite + Tailwind v4,
  react-router 7, Leaflet/OpenStreetMap. No paid APIs.

SOURCES VERIFIED (retrieved 2026-09-25)
---------------------------------------
- CDC Adult Immunization Schedule by Age, Notes, and Appendix (contraindications and
  precautions), all "Addendum updated July 2, 2025". US guidance changed during
  2025-26 and parts are under a legal stay (March 2026); rules follow what CDC
  currently publishes, and this is stated on the Sources page.
- CDC Vaccine Information Statements: influenza, Tdap, hepatitis B, MMR, varicella
  (all 2025-01-31), recombinant zoster (2022-02-04), HPV (2021-08-06), PCV (2025-05-29).
- India NHM National Immunization Schedule and the vaccine-wise NIS (Td-1 early in
  pregnancy, Td-2 four weeks later, or one booster if vaccinated within 3 years), and
  the MoHFW Td FAQ.

WHAT WAS BUILT (by phase)
-------------------------
1  Design system (design.md, OKLCH tokens, Source Serif 4 / Public Sans / IBM Plex Mono),
   layouts, route tree; removed fabricated content (fake "Live review", invented
   clinicians and licence numbers, pre-signed "verified" banner, invented versions,
   hidden client-side copy of medical rules).
2  Auth: sign-up, sign-in, sign-out, forgot/reset password; scrypt hashes; HttpOnly
   SameSite cookie sessions stored hashed; CSRF double-submit token; login rate limit;
   generic error messages. Signed-in users resume intake or go home.
3  Intake: profile (DOB, not age; pregnancy only when relevant), location/jurisdiction
   (selects the guideline set; optional approximate location to ~1 km), health.
4  Vaccination history (had it / haven't / not sure per vaccine, doses with exact,
   month, year or unknown dates, product types), structured safety screening
   (allergy triage + details, past reaction triage + details, 7 vaccine-specific
   safety questions), review page with Edit links and "Run vaccination assessment".
5  Versioned knowledge base: data/knowledge/us-cdc-adult (8 vaccines) and
   data/knowledge/in-uip (Td in pregnancy); validator refuses rules without sources.
6  Engine: backend/engine (facts, three-valued logic, series timing, evaluation);
   49 fixture tests covering ages, jurisdictions, unknowns, allergies, reactions,
   contraindications, precautions, multi-dose timing, doses given too early,
   delayed series, pregnancy windows, fail-closed cases.
7  Results page grouped by status, with "Why am I seeing this?" (factors, guideline
   basis, source link, rule references), missing-information lists, provenance line.
8  Vaccine pages: Overview, Safety (expected vs rare reactions, contraindications,
   reasons to wait, precautions, when to get help, "not proof it caused it" note),
   Schedule (can I have it now?, dose timeline, interval table, sources).
9  Providers: list first with optional map, availability states (available / limited /
   unavailable / not verified / no data) with last-checked dates, cost breakdown
   (vaccine + service fee = estimated total, or "Price not available"), save provider.
10 Record a dose -> after-care page; automatic re-assessment; reminders generated
   from structured timing; schedule page (done / dismiss / custom reminders);
   history (vaccinations, assessments, saved, change log with field names only).
11 Home: next action, dose timeline, safety notice, current recommendations,
   upcoming reminders, recent activity, quick links.
12 Accessibility (labels, fieldsets, focus rings, 44 px targets, skip links, text +
   icon statuses), responsive checks, security headers, data export and account
   deletion, end-to-end browser test, typography and copy polish.

KNOWN LIMITS / SKIPPED
----------------------
- No email or push notifications: reminders appear in the app; in demo mode the reset link is shown on screen.
- No real provider data source or booking integration exists; only labelled demo data.
- The provider map only appears after the user shares an approximate location; it
  wasn't covered by the automated test (the test user shares none).
- Travel vaccines, COVID-19, RSV, hepatitis A, meningococcal and mpox are not assessed
  in this version (stated on the Sources page).
- Medical rules should be reviewed by a qualified clinician before any real-world use.
- SQLite is not encrypted at rest; use disk encryption or a managed database for
  production, and set COOKIE_SECURE=true behind HTTPS.
- Old prototype files (frontend/src/pages/*Page.jsx, components/Navbar etc.,
  rule_engine/) are kept and reachable at /legacy; nothing was deleted without
  confirmation. App.css and assets/hero.png are unused leftovers.
